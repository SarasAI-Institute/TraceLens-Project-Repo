# Assessment and final demonstration

TraceLens Criterion Set **v1.1**, aligned with the current PromptLab course package.
The full criterion text is in [rubrics/CRITERIA.md](rubrics/CRITERIA.md);
[the module PDFs](rubrics/README.md) reproduce it. The Syllabus governs course policy.
No weekly capstone upload is required: retain the five module evidence groups for final review.

The final instructor-issued selection names one backend function and one React component at the final
commit. 48 hours from issue; 12–20 continuous screen/audio minutes, visible code, no cuts or other person
assisting. Explain what/why/removal-consequence for both items; trace UI→client→handler→storage→response→render;
defend a decision and show a traceable AI override and error/unhelpful incident with its cost. You may read
code. Up to three written questions clarify ambiguous answers, due in two business days. Re-attempts use
new files; accommodations may change format/time, not competence requirements.

The 22 criteria are defined in rubrics/CRITERIA.md and their evidence is tracked in EVIDENCE.md. No averaging:
all criteria per competency must be Met. C1.4, C2.4, C2.7, C3.3 and C3.6 are MUST PASS; failure returns
the affected evidence group. The five original module groups retain separate three-attempt accounts
within final review; returns do not consume attempts. Revision plans and >=48 hours after feedback are
required; consultation precedes a third attempt. Preserve authentic history rather than reconstructing it.

**Timing caveat:** the original four-hour scaffold-to-first-passing-backend-endpoint budget is unvalidated.
Collect pilot timestamps/blockers; require calibration and criterion versioning before timing-based Not Yet.
Do not automatically award Met for missing calibration. This is not a four-hour budget for the whole app.

## Review order and completeness

Every criterion is Met or Not Yet; all 22 must be Met for all three competencies to be Mastered. C1 completes with Module 2 evidence, C2 with Module 4 evidence, and C3 with Module 6 evidence. C2.1/C2.2 are provisional in Module 3 and finalized in Module 4; maintain them after Module 5 changes.

An unmet MUST PASS returns the affected evidence group before its remaining criteria are reviewed. Missing required evidence, failing provided tests, an application that will not run, or committed credentials also cause a return. Rotate any exposed credentials before resubmission. Work belonging to someone else or fabricated logs/history is referred for academic review. There are no bonus points or averaging; optional extensions cannot compensate for a missing required criterion.

See [the full criteria](rubrics/CRITERIA.md) and [cross-track map](COMPETENCY_MAP.md). The supplied red diagnostic baseline is expected **at the start**, but its tests must pass in completed Module 2 evidence and remain passing thereafter.
